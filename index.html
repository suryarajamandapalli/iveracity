System architecture

From sensor to screen in seconds.

A straightforward data path engineered for reliability, even in low-connectivity zones.

Sensors collect data

GPS and onboard sensors on each vehicle capture location, fuel, engine, and tyre data continuously.

GSM transmits it

The GSM module pushes readings to our central server in near real time, even on the move.

Server processes & stores

Data is validated, stored, and turned into alerts, trends, and reports automatically.

You see it instantly

Control room teams and field officers view live status on web dashboard or mobile app.
